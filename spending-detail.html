How to read the budget

Budget authority

Legal authority to commit funds.

→
This page

Obligation

A binding commitment, such as a contract or grant, made before cash is paid.

→

Outlay

Cash paid, including payments on earlier commitments.

Obligations and outlays can fall in different years: a multi-year grant obligated in FY2024 is paid out over several years, so a single year's outlays include old commitments. This page shows obligations — what each agency chose to commit — not cash outlays. And a drop in new authority alone doesn't prove a program ended: agencies can keep spending from prior-year balances.
